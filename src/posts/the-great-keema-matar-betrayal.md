<p>So here’s me.</p>

<p>48 hours ago, I was bravely surviving on liquids like some kind of spiritual detox influencer. Except this wasn’t wellness—this was <strong>post-endoplasty with one band installed</strong>. 🫠</p>

<p>The doctor said:<br>“Liquid diet for 48 hours.”</p>

<p>Me: <em>Fine. I am strong. I am resilient. I am basically soup now.</em></p>

<p>Then came the magical words:<br>“You can now start semi-liquids."</p>

<p>And suddenly I felt like I’d been upgraded from Economy to Premium Economy. Not business class. Relax.</p>

<hr />

<p>Naturally, my brain said:<br>“Keema matar?” 😍</p>

<p>My body said:<br>“Sir. You have a band in your esophagus. Please behave.”</p>

<p>Because apparently, semi-liquid does NOT mean:</p>

<ul>
<li>Chunky minced meat</li>

<li>Peas with attitude</li>

<li>Full masala Bollywood drama</li>
</ul>

<p>It means:</p>

<ul>
<li>Smooth.</li>

<li>Soft.</li>

<li>Emotionally stable food.</li>
</ul>

<p>Keema matar right now would be less “comfort food” and more “why are you like this?”</p>

<hr />

<h4>What My Life Looks Like Now</h4>

<p>I’ve entered my:</p>

<ul>
<li>Moong dal era</li>

<li>Thin khichdi phase</li>

<li>Blended soup personality</li>

<li>Mashed potato main character arc</li>
</ul>

<p>Everything must:</p>

<ul>
<li>Glide.</li>

<li>Not fight back.</li>

<li>Not require chewing like it’s a jaw workout challenge.</li>
</ul>

<p>Even curd has to be “not too sour.”<br>Even buttermilk must behave.<br>Even oats have to humble themselves.</p>

<hr />

<h4>Things I’m Avoiding Like Ex Texts</h4>

<ul>
<li>Spicy gravies</li>

<li>Fried food</li>

<li>Chunky meat</li>

<li>Citrus</li>

<li>Anything that says “let’s test this band.”</li>
</ul>

<p>Because right now, the goal is healing — not auditioning for “India’s Got Swallowing Problems.”</p>

<hr />

<h4>Conclusion</h4>

<p>Am I dreaming about keema matar? Yes.<br>Am I going to eat it? Not unless I want my doctor to personally unfollow me.</p>

<p>For now, I am one with Dal.<br>I am spiritually aligned with khichdi.<br>I respect the band.</p>

<p><strong>And one day… we shall reunite, dear Keema.</strong></p>

<p><strong>But today is not that day. 😌</strong></p>
