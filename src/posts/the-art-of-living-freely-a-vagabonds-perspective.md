<p>I’ve been in the UAE since 2007. Long enough for this place to stop being “abroad” and start being home. Long enough to build, rebuild, grow roots, create networks, watch skylines change, and seasons of life shift.</p>

<p>And yet—somewhere inside—I've always been a vagabond. </p>

<p>Not the lost kind.<br>Not the irresponsible kind.<br>The free kind.</p>

<p>There’s a difference.</p>

<p>Being a vagabond, for me, doesn’t mean running away from life. It means running toward it. It means trusting instinct over hesitation. It means if something feels aligned in the moment, I move. If it doesn’t, I don’t force it.</p>

<p>I’ve never been good at living on autopilot.</p>

<p>If I want to drive at midnight, I drive.<br>If I want silence, I disappear for a bit.<br>If I want to build something, I build it like this is my only shot at it.</p>

<p>Because maybe it is.</p>

<p>I live each day like it could be the last—not dramatically, not recklessly—but intentionally. I don’t like half-hearted conversations. I don’t like postponed dreams. “Someday” has never impressed me.</p>

<p>If it matters, I act.<br>If it excites me, I chase it.<br>If it drains me, I release it.</p>

<p>That’s the vagabond code.</p>

<p>People often mistake stability for routine. I don’t. Stability is internal. It’s knowing who you are, no matter where you are. I can stay in one country for decades and still have a wanderer’s spirit. The movement isn’t always geographical—sometimes it’s mental, emotional, or creative.</p>

<p>I follow curiosity.<br>I follow energy.<br>I follow the moment.</p>

<p>Life isn’t a rehearsal. There’s no practice round. It’s a one-take scene, and I refuse to play it safe just to look responsible on paper.</p>

<p>I’d rather have stories than perfectly structured plans.<br>I’d rather take the risk than wonder “what if.”<br>I’d rather feel everything than numb myself with predictability.</p>

<p>That’s not chaos. That’s aliveness.</p>

<p>Being a vagabond means I won’t always fit into neat boxes. It means I might pivot when others expect consistency. It means I prioritize experience over approval.</p>

<p>And honestly? I’m okay with that.</p>

<p>It’s not rebellion.<br>It’s not confusion.<br>It’s clarity.</p>

<p>This is me.<br>It’s who I am.</p>

<p>A rooted wanderer.<br>A grounded drifter.<br>A builder with a restless spirit.</p>

<p><strong>Living fully. Choosing freely.<br>Every single day.</strong></p>
