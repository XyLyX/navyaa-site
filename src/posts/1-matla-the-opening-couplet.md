<p>​Ab kisi ki zindagi ka mudda'a banna nahi<br />Apne hone ka kisi ko hausla banna nahi</p>

<p>​Meaning: I no longer wish to be the central purpose of anyone's life; I have no desire to force someone to find courage just to acknowledge my existence.</p>

<p>​2. The Core Philosophy</p>

<p>​Jis jagah rakh de koi, bas wahin qaaim raho<br />Band kamron ke liye tum ko hawa banna nahi</p>

<p>​Meaning: Stay firmly rooted right where you are naturally kept. You don't need to become a desperate gust of wind trying to force your way into locked rooms.</p>

<p>​3. On Self-Respect</p>

<p>​Maang kar paaya to phir us pyaar ki qeemat hi kya<br />Iltija ban kar kisi ki bad-du'a banna nahi</p>

<p>​Meaning: If love has to be begged for, it loses all its worth. Do not reduce yourself to a pleading supplication that eventually feels like a burden to them.</p>

<p>​4. Maqta (The Closing Couplet)</p>

<p>​Khud pukaare gi mohabbat, gar zaroorat ho use<br />Be-sabab har morr par tum ko sada banna nahi</p>

<p>​Meaning: Love will call out to you on its own if it truly needs you. You don't have to become an echoing voice waiting at every turn of their road.</p>
