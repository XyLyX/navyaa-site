<p>Sometimes the loudest pain makes no noise.<br>The soul cries, but the lips stay sealed.<br>You smile, you move, you survive—<br>while something deep inside quietly breaks.</p>

<p>You don’t explain anymore.<br>because explanations need energy, <br>and grief has already taken it all.<br>You tell yourself it’s fate,<br>that maybe this was written long before you felt it.</p>

<p>Acceptance doesn’t mean it didn’t hurt.<br>It just means you stopped asking <em>why</em>.<br>You carry the ache with grace.<br>turning wounds into silence<br>and silence into strength.</p>

<p>Some nights are heavier than others,<br>but you still whisper to yourself—<br>not as surrender,<br>but as survival:</p>

<p><strong>“Ye Rab di marzi hai.”</strong></p>

<p><strong>And somehow,<br>You keep going.</strong></p>
